## Marist Support 
---

##### School of Science Summer Research Awards
These are typically ~$4500 over the course of 8 weeks and are typically distributed via email. Upper-level students are the most competitive. Touching base with me to apply for this award is suggested in order to provide guidance on the scope of the project and workload.

## External Funding or Grant Opportunities
---

##### NSF REU (Research Experiences for Undergraduates) Program

Apply to participate in projects domestically or abroad in a broad range of disciplines.

[Link](https://www.nsf.gov/funding/initiatives/reu)

##### DOE SULI (Science Undergraduate Research Program) Internship

This application is similar to an REU but focuses on working at dedicated research facilities. This work will take on a more applied approach compared to basic science ares seen in the other programs.

[Link](https://science.osti.gov/wdts/suli)

##### NASA Internship/REU Programs

Another application with a broad range of opportunities. These can tend to be more competitive and having more research experience can help your chances of being selected.

[Link](https://www.nasa.gov/learning-resources/internship-programs/)
##### SETI Institute REU

This program is dedicated to more intensive astrobiological focused research opportinities and works directly with the NASA Ames Research Center. Research primarily spans between astrobiology, geology, and microbiology.

[Link](https://www.seti.org/education/reu/)

##### Marine Science REU's

Here are two different opportunities that focus mainly on marine science focused opportunities. These are not the only ones, but these have dedicated programs that include opportunities for cruise work.

[Link to Oregon State University Program](https://ceoas.oregonstate.edu/summer-research-internships-undergraduates)

[Link to University of Delaware Program](https://www.udel.edu/academics/colleges/ceoe/research/marine-sciences-summer-program/)

## Generally a great resource to navigate funding opportunities
---

##### Pathways to Science
A database to help search for different opportuniteis to support science and education training opportunities generally in STEM. Also supports a lot of other resources for navigating ways to receive support in different avenues of science.

[Link](https://www.pathwaystoscience.org/index.aspx)
